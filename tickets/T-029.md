## Context

After D-6 a K-1 captain owns exactly `swivel_gun` and `culverin`, both on `add_within_10`.
That skill is on **no island's** `rangeSkills`. Port Sumwich trains `add_within_20`,
`sub_within_20`, and `two_step_add_sub` — harder than the guns they hold. The youngest player
cannot practise the only skill their cannons ask.

Owner ruling **D-7** (2026-07-29): both halves, or neither — the skill joins the lane **and** a
cannon unlocks from mastering it. The one-liner (rangeSkills only) is forbidden: practice would
pay nothing, and fog would open Isla Products (multiplication) on a five-year-old's first mastery.

## Owner rulings recorded here

### D-7 content change

1. `add_within_10` joins `port_sumwich.rangeSkills`.
2. A **range-unlock** cannon whose `skill === 'add_within_10'` exists on Port Sumwich, so
   `resolveUnlocks` pays a cannon when the lane is mastered (not a starter — starters are
   placement, D-6).

### Fog decision (named, not discovered)

**Accepted: early Isla Products fog lift is harmless.**

`resolveUnlocks` still lifts an island when _any_ predecessor `rangeSkills` entry is mastered.
Mastering `add_within_10` therefore reveals Isla Products on the chart. That is **accepted**:
band-gating (placement skill/cannon grades, range content filters, cannon `minGrade`) still
governs what a K-1 captain is _served_ and which guns they can fire. Chart visibility ≠ content
availability. Tightening the predecessor rule is declined for this ticket — it would be a
mastery-semantics change beyond the pedagogy gap D-7 names.

### Invented payoff cannon (not in PLAN.md armory table)

| Field       | Value                                                | Note                                                                     |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| id          | `saker`                                              | **Invented** — historical light naval gun between Swivel and Six-Pounder |

The original design-review idea of a _third starter_ on `sub_within_10` is **deferred** — D-7
reframes T-029 as "can a K-1 child practise at all", and PLAN's "third cannon" is the range
unlock, not a third placement grant.
