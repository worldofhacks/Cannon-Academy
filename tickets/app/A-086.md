## Ruling

Amended D-17 + **D-13 binding** + board supersession: `Cannon Academy Uncharted Host.dc.html`
(project `88888c12-22e4-4781-b76f-a28110506499`) makes Lumen THE host of the whole region —
one host for every generated island, superseding the design doc's species-cycling note. Her
deep-water vignette is deliberately unlike the five beach hosts.

## The board is pixel authority — with two D-13 OVERRIDES

The board mock predates D-13's enforcement and contains two things that MUST NOT ship:

1. The "Grown-ups: skip the island chats" link — REMOVED everywhere by D-13. Do not build it.
2. Elliptical questions ("…then 3 more. How many?") — every rendered riddle closes with the
   full restated question: "…How many lamps did I light?" (spec(A-066:AC-7) clarity lint).

Everything else transcribes: Lumen's 9 shapes (body #4C7C9E/#2F5C7C, tail, 2 fins, 2 eyes,
mouth variants, carried lamp #FFD23F glow + rod #6B7F93 — lamp OUT TO THE SIDE, never over
the face, NO teeth ever), the five poses (greeting/asking/celebrating/shrugging/farewell)
with the board's mouth/arm/star tables, deep-water vignette (dark seabed, gold motes, her
lamp the only warm light), and the encounter-card chrome reusing the A-066 card layout
(FITTED bubble, 2×2 answer tiles at the duel's 34px numerals, amber-never-red shrug card,
coins-only reward).

## The encounter flow

`src/services/uncharted/encounter.ts`: Lumen greets on the FIRST frontier visit
(`uncharted.metLumen` latch via A-079's action), then ASKS on each generated island's
arriving→ready transition. Her riddle is the **tally riddle** — templated on real state,
numbers that grow with progress: lamps lit derived deterministically from (clearedCount,
island seed), answer always within the captain's band ceiling and computable as a single
in-band operation (k_1: sums within the band's rungs; g4_5 may scale). D-13 whole-question
closing line, validated by the same clarity predicate the authored riddles pass. Correct →
celebrating + small coin grant (receipted, idempotent, the encounter-reward pattern);
close → shrugging, amber, nothing lost; either way → ready state, SET SAIL live.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1**: Pose fidelity — the five poses' mouth/arm/star/anim tables pin to the board
  values exactly; the lamp never overlaps the face region; no tooth shape exists
  (source-scan on the figure).
- **AC-2**: D-13 holds — no skip affordance in any uncharted component (source-scan), and
  100 swept tally riddles all pass the clarity lint (trailing `?`, ≥5-word closing sentence
  restating the action) and stay within the FITTED bubble bounds.
- **AC-3**: The riddle grows but never breaches — for each band, swept across clearedCount
  0..30: every asked sum/answer sits under the band ceiling and the answer is among the 4
  distinct tiles exactly once.
- **AC-4**: Reward integrity — a correct answer grants coins exactly once per island
  (receipt-idempotent, double-tap safe); a wrong answer grants nothing, loses nothing, and
  the flow still reaches ready. The authored encounter suite
  (`__tests__/app/encounter.test.ts`) passes UNTOUCHED — `HOSTS`, `RIDDLE_POOLS`, and the
  enumeration never learn Lumen.
