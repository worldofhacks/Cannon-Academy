## Ruling

**D-14** (read it in `tickets/app/OWNER-RULINGS.md`), on top of A-069's landed catalog. Every
consumer that read the old shared `rangeSkills`/`displayName` now reads
`islandCurriculumFor(islandId, band)` — the captain's own band, the fail-closed way.

## The consumers (grep is your map — the old fields no longer compile)

- `engine/mastery.ts` — `advanceOnWin` + `resolveUnlocks`'s island eligibility and entry-cannon
  grant read the band's curriculum. Under the atlas every island is band-eligible by
  construction (A-069's validator guarantees it), but the gate STAYS — it is the tripwire that
  catches a future bad catalog at runtime, not just at import. Null band still fails closed.
- `engine/placement.ts` — placement still opens island one only; grant starters per D-6/D-9
  against the band's island-one curriculum.
- `services/chart.ts` — `chartNodes`/`chartProgress`/`islandGlyphForCaptain`: names and glyphs
  from the band's cells (glyph = first skill's glyph); progression copy unchanged (`NEXT: 1
  DUEL` while a next island exists — which is now every band until island five).
- `services/range.ts` + `trainingCatalog.ts` — drills list the band's skills for the island.
- `services/encounter.ts` — `encounterSkillFor` picks from the band's curriculum (its
  eligibility logic mostly collapses into "first skill", but keep the ceiling clamp).
- `services/rivalLoadout.ts` / `duelContext.ts` / `stores/duel.ts` — rival loadout derivation
  and any island-content reads go through the accessor; the duel's own question gate
  (`asksInBand` on the PLAYER loadout) is already band-true and stays untouched.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1**: **Every band reaches all five islands in exactly four wins**, driven through the
  real spine (`commitGradeBand` → four settled frontier wins) — the D-14 restatement of the
  old fixpoints. The everReachableIslands fixpoint = all five, at every band.
- **AC-2**: **The ceiling holds everywhere it used to, restated**: for every band × island,
  every drill skill, every duel-tray gun, every entry-cannon grant, and every encounter skill
  sits at or under the band's ceiling — the A-060 tripwire reborn as a 15-cell sweep. A K-1
  captain's rendered questions still never contain × or ÷, now proven on ALL FIVE of their
  islands.
- **AC-3**: Entry cannons arrive per band: each band's four arrivals each land exactly one
  in-band gun whose skill that island teaches THAT band, receipt-idempotent (the A-062 AC-3
  property swept across the atlas).
- **AC-4**: Placement is band-true: a fresh captain of each band holds island one, its
  band-correct starters (D-6/D-9 unchanged), and a tray that can fire on island one.
- **AC-5**: Null/corrupt band fails closed at every migrated site: no unlock, no drill, no
  encounter skill, no rival gun — swept.
- **AC-6**: The frozen suites in scope are re-baselined citing D-14 in each header — the old
  "K-1 reach is exactly two" / "g2_3 stops at Quotient Cove" specs become their five-island
  successors — and every OTHER frozen suite (duel-core, reducer, sprites, ship-skins,
  enemy-presentation, demo-navigation, fleet, guided-duel, onboarding-wiring) passes
  UNTOUCHED.
