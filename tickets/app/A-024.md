## Context

Placement pre-unlocks curriculum-appropriate islands, but the chart collapses every unfogged
non-current node into `cleared`, so a fresh grade 2–3 captain sees green ticks on islands never
mastered or visited. Separately, `Fog.tsx` lays a high-opacity rectangular SVG gradient over the
bottom of the map, which reads as a grey UI box rather than drifting weather. The reference names
four distinct states—cleared, current, available/near, and fogged—and draws fog without a visible
rectangular edge.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given placement-unlocked islands whose range skills are not mastered, when chart nodes
  are derived, then they are available and tappable but not cleared and receive no green tick.
- **AC-2**: Given an island whose full `rangeSkills` set is mastered, when it is not current, then
  it alone may render the cleared green-tick state; current always takes visual precedence.
- **AC-3**: Given a fogged island, when the chart renders, then its name/requirement remains
  readable and the fog is composed as edge-free weather without a rectangular SVG wash or opaque
  grey block across the dock boundary.
- **AC-4**: Given current, available, cleared, locked-near, and far-silhouette nodes, when they are
  rendered, then each has a distinct accessibility label and only unfogged nodes are tappable.

## Test Plan

- Unit (deterministic): node-state derivation from placement unlocks and mastery for AC-1/AC-2.
- Source/structure (deterministic): station-state exhaustiveness, labels/tappability, and no
  rectangular fog overlay for AC-3/AC-4.
- Native visual (release evidence): fresh grade 2–3 chart at 375×812 and 360×640, with no false
  checks or grey rectangle.
