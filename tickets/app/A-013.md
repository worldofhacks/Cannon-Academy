## Context

The design project ships **8 ship sprites, 6 flags, 2 crew and 2 dinghies**. The app uses none of
them: ships are composed from primitives because that is what the first pass reached for. Board 5a
is explicit that production ships are one pre-rendered sprite per hull with cosmetic layers stacked
at runtime.

This ticket also establishes the fidelity check itself, because "pixel perfect" is currently an
opinion and needs to be a measurement.

## Planning Decisions

- **A-013 is the owner of the presentation layer, not just of the sprites.** `src/theme/tokens.ts`,
  `responsive.ts`, `useLayout.ts`, `cannonPresentation.ts`, `src/components/Poly.tsx` and six of the
  seven `src/components/duel/*` files shipped in no ticket's `file_scopes` at all. They are the
  substrate every fidelity claim in this ticket is measured against — `design-fidelity.test.ts`
  already asserts against `tokens.ts` and is the mechanism the posture note calls the strongest of
  the three — so they belong here. `src/theme/flags.ts` and `shipCosmetics.ts` stay with A-006 and
  `src/components/duel/Panels.tsx` stays with A-010; scopes remain exclusive.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given the design project's sprite set, when the app builds, then every sprite it
  references resolves and is bundled.
- **AC-2**: Given the duel screen, when a ship renders, then it uses the pre-rendered sprite with
  cosmetic layers, not composed primitives.
- **AC-3**: Given the sprite manifest, when a referenced file is missing, then the build fails
  rather than rendering a blank.
- **AC-4**: Given the duel screen at 375pt, when compared to the board, then the geometry of the
  HUD, sea band and sheet match within 2pt.
- **AC-5**: Given the flag chosen at onboarding, when a ship renders, then it is the pennant.

## Test Plan

- Unit (deterministic): AC-1 and AC-3 — the manifest is typed and every entry can be resolved
  headless.
- Visual (design-fidelity, NOT frozen): AC-2, AC-4, AC-5 as a screenshot diff against the rendered
  board at the reference width. **Posture-gated deferral** — no component-test harness exists.
