## Ruling

**D-14** on top of A-069's catalog and A-070's migration (both land just before you — read all
three). You give the atlas its voice: the chart shows each band its own island names, and the
four new skills get encounter riddles that obey **D-13's whole-question rule**.

## The work

1. **Riddles for the four new rungs**, 3 each, host-voiced for the island that teaches them
   (per A-069's atlas: Take-Away Bay is k_1's island two → its host is Pip the parrot's
   position? NO — hosts are keyed by ISLAND ID and stay put: isla_products hosts Pip whatever
   the band calls it; write sub_within_10 riddles in Pip's voice, place_value_teens in Gale's,
   multi_digit_mult in Pip's, long_division in Tumble's). D-13 binding: every closing question
   restates the action in full and passes the spec(A-066:AC-7) clarity lint. Validated by
   templateSchema, 30-seed swept, ladder fill <25%. `riddles.ts` loads the new files.
2. **Chart presentation sweep**: A-070's services already hand chartNodes band-true names and
   glyphs — verify Station/Dock render them without truncation assumptions (the existing
   full-name-on-phone rules), and fix any hardcoded island-name width or copy that assumed the
   old five names. Longest new name is `Long-Divide Deep` / `Teen-Ten Harbor` — the A-049-era
   no-truncation contracts must hold at SE width.
3. **Encounter enumeration**: the suite's authored-coverage enumeration now demands riddle
   files for every skill any band's arrival can ask under the atlas — all four new rungs
   included, so a future atlas edit without riddles fails loudly.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1**: Twelve new riddles ship (3 × 4 rungs), each strict-parsed, seed-swept, and passing
  the D-13 clarity lint; the enumeration proves every atlas-askable skill has authored riddles
  and the fallback path is pinned for exactly the non-arrival skills.
- **AC-2**: Every band sees its own five names on the chart — pinned at the data level (the
  chartNodes projection for each band equals A-069's name table) and at the render level by
  the existing no-truncation/source-guard patterns at SE and Pro Max.
- **AC-3**: Glyph sanity: each band's island glyph is its first skill's glyph, and no k_1
  island ever shows × or ÷ on the chart — the islandGlyphForCaptain property swept across all
  15 cells.
- **AC-4**: The five hosts stay keyed by island id — same creature greets every band at a
  given position — pinned so a band swap can never orphan an encounter.

## Constraints

- D-13: no truncated questions, amber never red, numeric answers only.
- Do not touch services (A-070's), catalogs (A-069's), templates, or the duel.
- Riddle prose stays within the FITTED bubble comfortably (three lines at 19px max — the
  multi_digit_mult/long_division stories must stay short: "I pack {a} crates of {b} limes.
  How many limes do I pack?").

## Verification required

`npx tsc --noEmit` clean. Targeted single-fork: the two test_scopes files fully green.
Mutation checks: deleting one new riddle file must redden the enumeration; a truncated
question in a new file must redden the clarity lint; swapping one band's name cell must
redden AC-2.

## Out of scope

Catalog/schema (A-069); consumer migration (A-070); host art; new encounter states.
