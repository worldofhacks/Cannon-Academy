## Why

A-049 made `control.width` a ceiling so narrow phones shrink instead of clipping. That fixed phones
and exposed the opposite failure on wide screens: `maxWidth` caps each target, so the group stops
growing long before the column does, and the leftover was left-packed.

Measured before:

| screen | content column  | button group | empty to the right |
| ------ | --------------- | ------------ | ------------------ |
| 768    | 694pt, centred  | 365pt        | **328pt**          |
| 1280   | 1154pt, centred | 365pt        | **788pt**          |

Five buttons huddled at the left edge of a wide parchment band reads as a rendering error.

## Fix

`justifyContent: 'center'` on the control row. A no-op on phones — there is no free space to
distribute — and on tablet/desktop it centres the capped group under the centred column.

## Verified across the full range

| screen | horiz overflow | column centred | pad L / R     | target | meets 64pt floor |
| ------ | -------------- | -------------- | ------------- | ------ | ---------------- |
| 320    | no             | yes            | 11.3 / 11.3   | 52.5   | **no**           |
| 375    | no             | yes            | 12 / 12       | 62.2   | **no**           |
| 768    | no             | yes            | 164.2 / 164.2 | 69.7   | yes              |
| 1280   | no             | yes            | 394.2 / 394.2 | 69.7   | yes              |

Labels intact at every width. `tsc` clean, eslint clean, 2,309 passing with the same 16 pre-existing
failures HEAD produces.

Note on the earlier tablet report: the black surround in a tablet screenshot is the preview pane's own
letterboxing, **not** a layout bug — measured in CSS pixels the app root is exactly 768×1024 at 0,0
with no overflow. Measure, do not read the screenshot canvas.

## Still open — the 64pt floor on phones

Five controls cannot meet the boards' 64pt target floor at any phone width. Dropping to **four**
does, everywhere: at 375 each becomes 64.5 with 57pt of slack; at 320, 64.5 with slack. That is the
arithmetic behind the Harbor-button question put to the owner.
