## The four new skills (★), Common-Core anchored

1. `sub_within_10` — g0–1 (K.OA.2 / 1.OA.6). Subtraction within 10, non-negative answers.
2. `place_value_teens` — g1–1 (1.NBT.2/1.NBT.3). Teens as ten-and-ones: "1 ten and {a} ones",
   "10 + {a}", "how many ones in 1{a}" forms — integer answers, no symbol beyond +.
3. `multi_digit_mult` — g4–4 (4.NBT.5). 2–3-digit × 1-digit, and tens × tens.
4. `long_division` — g4–5 (4.NBT.6 / 5.NBT.6). 2–3-digit ÷ 1-digit, exact (integer) quotients.

Each ships the FULL way, matching every existing convention:
- 8 templates per file, params/constraints/answerExpr/3 declared distractors, the closed
  expression grammar only; seed-swept in the golden suites (no throws, ladder fill <25%).
- displayName, description, and glyph-ready phrasing consistent with `skills.json` neighbors.
- One entry cannon each in `cannons.json` (`{kind:'range', island:<its atlas island>, tier:1}`),
  minGrade = the skill's minGrade, timer/damage in family with same-grade peers:
  `dinghy_gun` (sub_within_10, g0), `teen_lantern` (place_value_teens, g1),
  `carronade` (multi_digit_mult, g4), `stern_chaser` (long_division, g4).

## Schema change

`islandSchema`: geometry/chain fields stay band-neutral (`id`, `requiresIsland`); the taught
content moves under `curriculum: { k_1: {...}, g2_3: {...}, g4_5: {...} }`, each
`{ displayName: string, skills: SkillId[≥1], unlocksCannons: CannonId[≥1] }`, strict. Keep a
top-level neutral `id`-derived key only — no shared `rangeSkills`/`displayName` remain, so
every consumer is FORCED through the accessor A-070 introduces (a leftover shared field would
let a call site silently keep the old world). Export the accessor here so content and its
reading stay one module: `islandCurriculumFor(islandId, band): { displayName, skills,
unlocksCannons }` — band `null` falls back to `g4_5` content for legacy/fixture callers,
documented as such.

**The D-14 ceiling validator** — the point of the ticket: `validateCatalogs` gains the law
*no island may carry, for any band, a skill whose minGrade exceeds that band's ceiling*
(k_1→1, g2_3→3, g4_5→5), and its dual: *every band's every island teaches at least one skill
NOT outgrown by that band* (skill.maxGrade ≥ the band floor: 0, 2, 4 respectively — so a
grade-5 captain is never sent to an island of pure kindergarten review). Both throw at import
with the island id, band, and skill named. Every unlocksCannons entry's skill must be on that
island-band's skill list.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1**: The atlas table above is the shipped catalog byte-for-byte — every island×band
  cell's skills and names — pinned in the content suite as a table-driven sweep.
- **AC-2**: The ceiling validator: a synthetic catalog putting `mult_facts` on a k_1 island
  throws at parse naming all three (island, band, skill); the not-outgrown dual throws on a
  g4_5 island of only g0–1 skills; the shipped catalog passes both for all 15 cells.
- **AC-3**: All four new skills generate: 30-seed sweep per template — zero
  CONSTRAINTS_UNSATISFIED, zero DISTRACTOR_FAILURE, ladder fill <25%, and every rendered
  `place_value_teens`/`sub_within_10` prompt contains no ×/÷ glyph (the K-1 print-safety
  sweep extended to the new files).
- **AC-4**: Every island×band has a valid entry-cannon path: the first in-band cannon in its
  `unlocksCannons` exists, fires a skill on that island-band's list, and its minGrade clears
  the band — swept over all 15 cells.
- **AC-5**: `SKILL_IDS` grows by exactly the four; every existing frozen content contract
  (template golden suites for existing files, cannon/enemy/rank schemas) passes untouched.
