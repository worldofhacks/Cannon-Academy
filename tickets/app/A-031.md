## Context

Every duel currently labels the opponent “Rival” and paints the same purple ship. The supplied
design artifact defines a low-cost roster by island: pirates, skeletons, ghost ship, shark crew,
and kraken, primarily through tint/opacity and a few primitive layers.

## Planning Decisions

- **[locked-decision]** Enemy identity is catalog data keyed by island and validated strictly.
- **[locked-decision]** Presentation changes do not alter engine damage or math. Hull continues to
  come from A-029's context.
- **[locked-decision]** Art follows the existing toy-like primitives and palette; no network asset
  or new runtime dependency is added.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given every island, when an encounter is selected, then it resolves a validated enemy
  with a distinct display name, faction, accessibility description, and presentation kind.
- **AC-2**: Given first through fifth island encounters, when rendered, then pirate/skeleton/ghost/
  shark/kraken identities are visually and textually distinguishable without color alone.
- **AC-3**: Given ghost and kraken presentations, when rendered, then the ghost uses the documented
  opacity/glow treatment and the kraken uses friendly pink/tentacle forms rather than a generic
  purple ship.
- **AC-4**: Given any enemy presentation, when duel mechanics run, then question, player cannon,
  payout, and damage rules are unchanged.
- **AC-5**: Given an unknown enemy/island reference, when catalogs validate, then startup/tests fail
  loudly rather than showing the generic rival.

## Test Plan

- Catalog/schema (deterministic): strict records, referential integrity, full island coverage.
- Presentation/source guards: all kinds have independent text/shape channels and no fallback.
- Native screenshots: at least pirate, ghost, and kraken at 375pt.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Tests are RED, independently reviewed, and frozen before implementation.
- [ ] Direction: bright toy-nautical; depth: plank edges; spacing: 4pt grid.
- [ ] No new dependency and no mechanics in presentation files.
- [ ] Local gates, screenshots, code review, and Security Agent review pass.

## Out of Scope

Signature boss attacks, skeleton-specific mechanics, merchant rescues, or additional downloaded art.
