## Why

Owner ruling, 2026-07-29: **pull the chest from the Harbor shelf entirely.**

The shipped screen contradicted itself. It sold a chest that `chestSettlement` can fill with a
**cannon**, under copy reading _"Earn cannons by learning — not by buying them."_ Coins bought
capability. The Harbor board forbids it outright:

> "Never put a cannon, a timer bonus, or a hull upgrade on this shelf. The moment coins buy power,
> cut the screen."

Nothing was orphaned by the removal: chests remain a **victory** reward through `settleDuelRewards`,
which is where the one chest-exclusive cannon is still earned. Verified by tracing both call sites
before the change.

## Why there are no purchase receipts

A-033 needed the `purchase:<sequence>` ledger because a chest outcome was **random** — a replay had
to prove it had not rerolled. A skin purchase is deterministic, so **ownership is the receipt**. A
second tap finds the skin owned and returns without debiting: idempotence by construction rather than
by bookkeeping, and it cannot drift out of step with a ledger because there is no ledger.

## Affordability never rests on colour

The board's requirement, implemented as three channels, none of them hue:

1. **The price tab is present or absent.** An unaffordable card simply has no tab.
2. **The card is raised or sunk** — white with a plank shadow, or flat parchment.
3. **The coin meter is a countable length** — the same language as the hull bar.

A colour-blind seven-year-old can separate the two states from the tab alone.

## Superseding A-033

`__tests__/app/harbor.test.ts` is replaced rather than amended, and says so at the top. The ruling
postdates the tests — the same adjudication the project made for D-8 — and the product they describe
no longer exists: `harborCatalog` and `buyHarborChest` are gone, not changed.

Carried over and still asserted: no real-money language, the 64pt purchase target, no cannon on the
shelf, idempotent repeat taps, the balance being coins and nothing else. Deliberately not carried
over: anything about chests, rarity rolls or receipts.

## Also

`successDeep` (`#1E7F41`) is now a named token. Measured both ways — white on it is 5.04, and it as
text is 5.04 on white and 4.69 on parchment — so it is the green that may carry a word, where
`success` may not (white on it is 2.63, a board-banned pair). Same discipline as A-054's `seaDeep`.
