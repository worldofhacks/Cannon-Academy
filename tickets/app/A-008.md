## Context

`computeCoinPayout` runs at the end of every duel and its result is rendered once and thrown away.
Mastery is never touched — `mastery.ts` is a complete engine module with zero app callers. Wins are
not counted, so `rankTierForWins` has nothing to read.

This is the ticket that turns the duel from a demo into a loop.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given a completed duel, when it resolves, then the coin payout is added to the captain
  and survives relaunch.
- **AC-2**: Given correct answers during a duel, when it resolves, then the matching skill's
  mastery rises at the duel rate — half the range rate, per PLAN.
- **AC-3**: Given a mastery crossing during a duel, when it resolves, then the unlocked cannon is
  granted and announced to the player.
- **AC-4**: Given a win, when it resolves, then `wins` increments and rank tier is re-derived.
- **AC-5**: Given a loss, when it resolves, then a smaller purse is still paid, rank does not drop,
  and hull is reset for the next duel.
- **AC-6**: Given a duel resolving twice from a re-render, when the outcome is recorded, then the
  reward is applied exactly once.

## Test Plan
