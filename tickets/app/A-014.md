## Context

`src/services/questions.ts` is a stopgap I wrote so the duel screen had something to ask before the
engine's generator existed. Its own header says it has no template pool, no recency window and no
distractor plausibility rules, and that it must not reach a child.

The engine side is now complete — T-007 landed the generator and T-014/T-015/T-016 landed the
templates — and **nothing in the app calls any of it.** Every question in the build today is fake.

The stopgap was written with T-007's signature deliberately, so this should be a swap rather than a
rewrite.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given a skill and an rng, when a question is requested, then it comes from the engine's
  generator and its template pool — not from a literal in the app layer.
- **AC-2**: Given the same seed and skill, when two questions are drawn, then they are identical —
  the replay property survives the swap.
- **AC-3**: Given a sequence of draws for one skill, when the recency window applies, then a
  recently-served template is not immediately re-served.
- **AC-4**: Given any generated question, when its choices are inspected, then there are exactly
  `CHOICE_COUNT` of them, the answer appears exactly once, and no distractor equals the answer.
- **AC-5**: Given the app layer, when it is searched, then `src/services/questions.ts` no longer
  contains its own arithmetic — no `a + b`, no hand-built distractor ladder.

## Test Plan

- Unit (deterministic): every AC. The generator is pure and seeded, so all of this runs headless.
