## Context

`createBotOpponent`, grade accuracy bands, loss-streak mercy, and forced misfires are complete but
have no app caller. The live presentation reducer instead draws flat 7–12 damage synchronously.

## Planning Decisions

- **[locked-decision]** The screen driver owns async work and stamps requests with a turn token.
  Stale or post-unmount promises are discarded.
- **[locked-decision]** The pure presentation reducer consumes a completed rival volley event; it
  never awaits and never owns a clock.
- **[locked-decision]** Mercy uses A-041's persisted field and is committed through one Captain
  replacement at answer/result boundaries; its tuned policy remains engine-owned.
- **[locked-decision]** Rival loadout is the stable catalog-order set whose cannon skill belongs to
  the current island and whose `minGrade` is within the captain's band. A valid unlocked island
  must yield at least one cannon; invalid/empty context fails closed instead of using a generic
  fallback.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given a captain grade band and mercy state, when a rival is created, then its accuracy
  and forced misfires come from `targetBotAccuracy`, the grade band, and existing engine constants.
- **AC-2**: Given the same seed, context, and action log, when the rival acts, then cannon choice,
  correctness, elapsed time, and resulting damage are replayable.
- **AC-3**: Given two rival turns resolving out of order, when the stale promise completes, then it
  produces no state transition or damage.
- **AC-4**: Given two consecutive losses, when the next duel starts, then the existing mercy policy
  grants its forced misfires; a win resets the loss streak according to engine behavior.
- **AC-5**: Given a rival miss, when it resolves, then player hull is unchanged and the UI reports
  the miss rather than inventing damage.
- **AC-6**: Given unmount or leaving the duel, when a pending rival promise completes, then no store
  or React state is updated.
- **AC-7**: Given every band-safe unlocked island, when its rival loadout is derived, then it is
  non-empty, deterministic, contains only age-eligible island skills, and is passed unchanged to
  both `createBotOpponent` and the canonical DuelConfig.

## Test Plan

- Unit/integration (deterministic): injected promises, stale-token orderings, seeded replay,
  grade bands, mercy lifecycle, rival-loadout coverage, and miss/hit paths.
- Native smoke: observe one rival miss and one hit without runtime warnings.
