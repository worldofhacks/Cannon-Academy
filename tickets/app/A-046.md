## Why

Owner ruling D-11, 2026-07-29: _"Ignore and overwrite any other design decisions you see and call
them out so i can delete them."_

A raw hex sweep of `src/**` and `app/**` against the full text of both artifacts (markup plus the
data island) found **22 colours that appear nowhere in either board**. Sixteen are replaced here; six
are escalated rather than changed, for a reason recorded below.

Clean before this ticket, and worth stating because it bounds the problem: `src/theme/tokens.ts` (48
colours), `src/components/chart/palette.ts` (11) and `src/components/chart/board.ts` all trace
100% to the artifacts. The invented colour was concentrated in one file.

## Escalated, not changed — `src/theme/flags.ts`

The six onboarding flag colours (`#E03131`, `#F59F00`, `#2F9E44`, `#0B8A8A`, `#1C7ED6`, `#9C36B5`)
are invented. The board's Name-and-flag screen authors its own six: `#F5A623`, `#2FB65E`, `#1584B8`,
`#D93A2E`, `#6C4BD6`, `#14283C`.

**Swapping to the board's palette would fail A-006's frozen accessibility test**, measured:

```
sea  #1584B8  hue 199.1
ink  #14283C  hue 210.0     hue gap 10.9 deg  vs a 25 deg floor  -> FAIL
```

The board can afford that because its flags carry two more channels: a **shape** (swallowtail vs
rectangle) and a **mark** (circle, triangle, star, cross, diamond). Our flags are colour-only, so the
hue floor is the only thing standing between a colour-blind five-year-old and six identical pennants
— which the design brief forbids outright.

So this is not a tint swap. It is "adopt the board's shape+mark flag design", which is an A-006
rework with frozen tests attached. **Owner decision required.** Until then the invented-but-accessible
palette stands, and that trade is deliberate.

## Acceptance criteria
