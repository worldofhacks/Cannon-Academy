## Reported

> "the questions in the duels must align with the level that the user picks in the beginning"

A-051 closed two places where the ceiling only **showed** — the gun-deck operator row and the chart's
green check. Neither touched the duel, and the duel is where a child is actually asked.

## What was actually true

Nothing in the app checked the band at duel time. `duelContext.ts` validates island access and never
reads `gradeBand`; `stores/duel.ts` handed the engine `captain.equippedCannons` whole. The ceiling was
carried entirely by **acquisition** gates — `resolvePlacement`, `resolveUnlocks`, `deriveRivalLoadout`,
`range.openDrill` — and every one of them had to remember.

One did not. `settleDuelRewards` → `rollChestSettlement` → `missingChestOnlyCannon` grants the first
chest-only cannon the captain does not own, with no band check on the path. That cannon is
`nine_pounder`, whose skill `place_value_compare` is `minGrade: 2`.

So: place at K–1, win one duel, take the chest, put the gun on the deck — `commitLoadout` refuses only
what is not OWNED — and the duel asks **"How many tens are in 807?"** to a five-year-old. Reproduced by
driving the real path (`commitGradeBand` → `settleDuelRewards` → `commitLoadout` → `duelReducer`), not
by reading the code.

**× and ÷ specifically were NOT reachable at K–1.** The chest can only grant `nine_pounder`, and the
multiplication and division guns are `range` unlocks whose skills a K–1 captain can never be served —
`resolveUnlocks` will not open Isla Products at that band, and `openDrill` refuses the skill. The
ceiling still leaked; the reported symptom was the one hole it did not leak through.

## Fix

One rule, `asksInBand` / `inBandLoadout` in `services/loadout.ts`, applied where a duel's questions are
CHOSEN rather than at each acquisition site — so a grant path added tomorrow inherits it without
knowing it exists:

- `stores/duel.ts` `legacyConfig` filters `playerLoadout` (and its empty-equipped catalog fallback,
  which used to hand a kindergartner every gun in the game). This is the hard gate: `selectCannon`
  refuses any `CANNON_SELECTED` outside `playerLoadout`.
- `app/duel.tsx` filters the rendered tray with the same rule, or a dropped gun becomes a dead tile in
  `select` — a phase with no other exit (the A-047 failure, one screen over).

The ceiling measures the SKILL's `minGrade`, not the cannon's, because the skill is what decides the
maths on the screen. A missing band fails **closed**; `engine/mastery.ts` reads an absent band as
`POSITIVE_INFINITY`, which is safe there only because a skill must be mastered before it unlocks
anything.

`defaultLegacyCaptain` now declares the top band instead of deriving one from the island. It equips the
whole catalog, so the top band is the only band consistent with its own guns — the derived band made it
a captain whose declared level contradicted its loadout, which is exactly what the new ceiling exists to
resolve, and it would have resolved it by deleting six cannons from a fixture whose purpose is to hold
all eleven.

**Replay is untouched.** `templatePools.ts` warns that file order is part of the contract because the
generator indexes into the pool it is handed. The ceiling drops whole CANNONS; it never subsets or
reorders a skill's template array, and `templatesBySkill` is still handed over as `TEMPLATE_POOLS`
entire. An in-band gun at a given seed draws exactly the question it drew before.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1**: Every question a real duel asks a captain placed through `commitGradeBand` is inside their
  band — asserted on the question's skill and rendered text, at every band, on every island that band
  has unlocked. A K–1 duel renders no `×` or `÷`.
- **AC-2**: The ceiling holds however the gun reached `equippedCannons` — chest grant, legacy save,
  `replaceCaptain`. Swept over every out-of-band cannon at every band, plus the reported chest path end
  to end. The survivors are exactly the in-band subset in the captain's own order — removal, never
  substitution. A captain with no band is handed nothing.
- **AC-3**: The guided first duel — its own loadout path, and the first duel any child sees — asks in
  band at every band, and refuses an out-of-band gun a save arrives carrying.
- **AC-4**: The rival's loadout is in band, and a rival volley never puts a question on screen at all.
- **AC-5**: Replay is preserved: an in-band cannon at a given seed draws exactly what `nextQuestion`
  produces from the whole file-ordered pool. Every band keeps something to fire, including a gun whose
  skill sits exactly ON its ceiling (a K–1 captain's earned `six_pounder`).

## Known residue

A K–1 captain can still **own** an out-of-band chest cannon and put it on the gun deck; the duel simply
never arms it and never shows it in the tray. Making the deck itself stop offering it is a gun-deck
display change, deliberately out of scope here — the pedagogy guarantee lives at the duel, where the
questions are chosen.
