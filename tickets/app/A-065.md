## The beats (board timings are the contract)

- **A · Sailing, 0→1800ms** — the existing sail, upgraded: hull leans ~18° along the tangent
  (+6° over resting), NO bob loop while under way; three white wake lozenges behind the stern
  (22×7 @ .8, 17×6 @ .5, 12×5 @ .26) scaling down and sliding back; trail dots light gold
  (6→7px, cream .45 → gold 1.0 with glow) as the hull passes each — lit behind, never ahead.
  Destination stays fogged, its marker slate. One bright thing: the ship.
- **B · Fog lift, 1800→3080ms** — ship settles into its bob (stillness hands attention over).
  Three overlapping steps: fog disc splits into two half-discs sliding ±26px + fade (0–420ms);
  color floods sand/grass from the desaturated locked palette via a circular reveal expanding
  from the island centre (300–780ms — RN has no clip-path: an expanding circular MASK view or
  scaled colored copy under `overflow:hidden` is the sanctioned equivalent, one animated value);
  gold marker pops with spring overshoot (380ms cubic-bezier(.2,1.4,.4,1), 480ms delay) + one
  620ms spark ring, then the standing 1.8s pulse. Dock island name + meter swap at this moment.
- **C · Banner, 3080→4600ms** — parchment banner slides from the left edge: in 380ms with
  overshoot, dwell ~1000ms, out 380ms. Geometry in the 375 frame: left 12, right 66, top 96 —
  never overlapping header (26–78), ship, or dock; island glyph plate left, name + "New waters,
  Captain!" + 44pt speaker slot right; gold spine, NO ring on the banner. The dock's Fight
  button turns gold at the same instant and takes the only ring.
- **D · Handoff, 4600→5200ms** — banner tucks out (translateX −30 + scale .86 + fade, 320ms,
  starting 80ms before the iris); spyglass iris closes on the island: aperture circle shrinking
  140%→0% at 50%/46%, 600ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.7,1), brass rim tracking the edge, scene visible
  THROUGH the hole (one dark surround with a circular aperture — in RN a huge-border circle
  view is the standard trick), header/dock fade to 35%. The duel opens from the iris.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1**: The four beats run in board order with board timings on a genuine arrival; a plain
  travel tap keeps today's bare sail (no fog lift, no banner, no iris, no battle).
- **AC-2**: The banner's frame geometry holds at SE (320pt-class) and Pro Max: never overlaps
  header pill, ship berth, or dock — asserted as arithmetic in the sail/ceremony test the way
  A-063's bounds spec does it.
- **AC-3**: One glow per beat, exactly as the board's amber card states it — ship (A), marker
  (B), Fight button (C), never two rings; the sailing ship runs no bob and the resting ship
  runs no lean.
- **AC-4**: A tap anywhere from beat C onward skips to the handoff; the skip and the natural
  path both end in exactly one battle push (double-fire discipline preserved from A-063).
- **AC-5**: The encounter slot: when `captain.seenEncounters` does not include the arrived
  island, the ceremony mounts `EncounterCard` (A-066's component: `{ islandId, onDone }`)
  between banner-out and iris, and `onDone` leads into the iris; when already seen, banner-out
  leads straight to the iris. The onboarding walkthrough overlay is untouched either way.
- **AC-6**: Worklet discipline: every new `useAnimatedStyle` is added to
  `CURRENT_SHIPPED_WORKLETS` by name; no worklet closes over a JS function; the ceremony is
  driven by shared values + timeouts in the chart screen, StrictMode-safe.
