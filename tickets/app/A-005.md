## Context

Board 1a is built and renders correctly, but the chosen band goes nowhere: it sets component state
and pushes to `/duel`. `resolvePlacement` is never called with it, so the pre-unlocked cannons and
islands the whole placement design exists to grant are never granted.

This ticket also lands A-003's resolver in `_layout`, which is what stops a returning captain
seeing the title screen.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given a band is chosen, when the choice is committed, then `resolvePlacement` is called
  with that band and its `unlockedCannons` and `unlockedIslands` are written to the captain.
- **AC-2**: Given a committed choice, when the app relaunches, then the same band and unlocks are
  present.
- **AC-3**: Given hydration completes, when the root layout renders, then the destination comes
  from the flow resolver and from nowhere else.
- **AC-4**: Given a 360×640 screen, when the picker renders, then all three cards meet
  `MIN_TAP_TARGET` and no content is clipped.

## Test Plan

- Unit (deterministic): AC-1, AC-2, AC-3 against the store and resolver — the wiring is logic and
  is testable headless.

- Visual (design-fidelity, NOT frozen): verified against the named board at the 375pt reference
  width, and at 360×640 / 390×844 / 430×932. Recorded as a screenshot diff in the ticket, reviewed
  by a second agent. **This is a posture-gated deferral**: the app layer has no component-test
  harness, so screen geometry cannot be frozen-tested today (see `.tdd-swarm/posture.md`).
