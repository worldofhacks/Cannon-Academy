## Why

The designer found a bug class in their own boards and named the root cause exactly:

> "The contrast table certifies **fills**, not text roles — so three colours got used as text on
> grounds nobody ever measured them against. Each was picked by proximity, not by certification.
> That's why per-instance fixes kept surfacing new instances."

We had the same class, shipping. Measured, not guessed — and all at 10–11px, where the "large text
only" allowance does not apply:

| Where                         | Pair                           | Ratio                                           |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `Panels.rewardTag`            | `goldDeep` on sunken parchment | **2.99** hard fail                              |
| `TemperBadge` reliable        | white on `success` green       | **2.63** — one of the board's two explicit bans |
| `rank.tsx` subtitle           | `inkFaint` on the dark surface | 3.49                                            |
| `QuestionPanel.fastIsPerfect` | `goldDeep` on parchment        | 3.56                                            |
| `TemperBadge` standard        | white on `sea`                 | 4.18                                            |

The fifth was found **by the test**, not by the sweep that motivated it: I checked the three colours
the designer named, wrote a measuring test, and it immediately failed on a pair I had not thought to
look at — the "steady" badge, which is the temperament a beginner meets first and sees most.

## Fixes

- **`goldDeep` retired as a text colour.** It is a plank shadow and stays valid as a fill. Both text
  sites move to `goldDeepest`, which already existed and measures 6.70 on parchment and 5.63 on sunken
  parchment — so the gold hue survives rather than flattening to ink.
- **`inkFaint` → `inkSoft`** on the dark surface: 3.49 → 6.00.
- **`sea` → `seaDeep`** for the standard temperament badge: white goes from 4.18 to 7.09.
- **The badge glyph colour is no longer a constant.** A single shared ink cannot be right for three
  different grounds — white is correct on the red and the deep blue (4.57 and 7.09) and banned on the
  green (2.63). `temperLook` now carries a certified `ink` per temperament, and `TemperBadge` renders
  it.
- **`seaDeep` is now a named token.** `#0C5E86` was inlined in five places, which is precisely how the
  badge ended up on the lighter `sea` — the readable blue had no name to reach for.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1**: Every text/ground pair the app renders clears AA 4.5.
- **AC-2**: The banned pairs still measure below AA — the arithmetic is asserted, so a retuned token
  fails a test rather than silently legalising a ban.
- **AC-3**: `goldDeep` and `inkFaint` never appear after `color:`.
- **AC-4**: The standard temperament badge uses the readable blue.

## Verification

`tsc` clean, eslint clean, 2,331 passing (4 new) with the same 16 pre-existing failures HEAD produces.

The test **measures** rather than greps, so the next colour swap is checked by arithmetic instead of
by whether someone remembered the rule. That is the whole point: the designer's diagnosis was that
proximity was standing in for certification, and a rule that lives only in prose is proximity again.
