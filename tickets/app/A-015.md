## Context

`app/guided-duel.tsx` is a 23-line stub written only to stop `flow.ts` routing a captain into a
missing route: it marks the step complete and redirects to the chart. A child finishing onboarding
today is shown nothing at all.

**This ticket was missing from the plan entirely.** It appeared only as a destination string in
A-003. The adversarial plan review the skill calls for — the step that finds "a requirement no
ticket traces to" — was not run, and this is what it would have caught.

The engine side is ready and unused: T-018 ships `createScriptedOpponent`, and
`ONBOARDING_ENEMY_HULL = 28` is tuned so the sloop politely sinks in three volleys against the
Swivel Gun's guaranteed floor.

**The owner's framing, which governs the scope:** the audience is kindergartners, so this is not a
difficulty setting — it is the only place the mechanic is ever taught. A child who leaves this
screen not knowing that _answering the question is what fires the cannon_ has not been taught
anything, however gently they won.

## Planning Decisions

- **A-039 owns the only hull rule and configuration seam.** Its canonical engine `DuelConfig`
  carries `enemyMaxHull` and `playerHullFloor`; this ticket only passes
  `ONBOARDING_ENEMY_HULL` and a floor of `1` through `openGuidedDuel`. It does not edit or clamp
  hull in `src/stores/duel.ts`, which remains a presentation-only adapter.
- **Rewards are a dependency, not a scope widening.** `src/services/duelRewards.ts` is A-008's file
  and needs no edit: `applyDuelOutcome` keys its ledger on `duelId`, `duelId` is derived from the
  seed, and the guided duel mints a fresh seed like any other. AC-5 is satisfied by _calling_ it.
  If an implementer finds they need to edit that file, the ticket is wrong and should come back to
  the planner rather than reach across scopes.
- **Not split into two tickets.** The plan review asked whether routing and the unlosable duel are
  separate concerns. They were — but the routing half was AC-1/AC-7, and both are deleted below as
  duplicates, which leaves one concern and one screen. Splitting a five-AC ticket that owns one
  route and one reducer option would cost a wave and buy nothing three days from submission.
- **AC-1 and AC-7 of the previous draft are deleted, not moved.** "The guided duel is the
  destination after onboarding, exactly once" and "a captain who has fought it is never routed back"
  are verbatim A-003:AC-4, which is implemented in `src/services/flow.ts` and carries a tagged
  frozen test. Restating another ticket's criterion makes it look like new work, and makes a change
  to `flow.ts` fail in two places with two owners.
- **The old AC-5 (correct answer shown before the turn passes) was misfiled as visual.** It is a
  reducer-state assertion — the `miss` and `timeout` phases hold for `PHASE_DURATION_MS` carrying
  the correct answer — and it runs headless. It is AC-4 below, in the deterministic row. The
  posture-gated visual row keeps only the coaching affordance's _appearance_.
- **The service seam is now explicit rather than left for the Test Agent to invent.**
  `openGuidedDuel(seed) -> { session, opponent }` creates A-039's canonical core/presentation
  session with the guided config and T-018 scripted opponent.
  `settleGuidedDuel(store, session) -> DuelRewardOutcome` delegates to A-032's durable settlement,
  marks the guided step complete only for a finished guided victory, and is idempotent. The screen
  keeps rendering the captain's equipped cannon tray; this ticket does not force or return a
  Swivel Gun merely because that cannon is used to explain the three-volley tuning bound.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given the guided duel, when it starts, then the rival is T-018's scripted opponent and
  its hull is `ONBOARDING_ENEMY_HULL` read from `@engine/tuning` — no hull, damage or accuracy
  literal appears in the app layer.
- **AC-2**: Given any finite sequence of correct, wrong, and timed-out turns — including prefixes
  containing only wrong answers or timeouts — then after every transition the player's hull stays
  above zero and the duel never reaches `defeat`. An all-wrong or all-timeout sequence may continue
  indefinitely; once enough correct volleys eventually sink the rival, the only terminal phase is
  `victory`. Losing the first duel is the worst first impression this game can make.
- **AC-3**: Given the guided duel is fought to its end, when it resolves, then
  `hasFoughtGuidedDuel` is true, it survives relaunch, and the captain's next destination is the
  sea chart.
- **AC-4**: Given a wrong answer or a burned fuse in the guided duel, when the turn resolves, then
  the resolving phase carries the correct answer and holds for its full `PHASE_DURATION_MS` before
  advancing — the teaching moment is not skippable here.
- **AC-5**: Given the guided duel resolves, when rewards are applied, then `applyDuelOutcome` runs
  against the captain store exactly once for this `duelId` and credits coins, per-skill mastery and
  the win on the same terms a real duel does — a tutorial that pays differently teaches the
  tutorial.

## Test Plan

- Unit (deterministic): AC-1, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5. The scripted opponent is seeded and the reward path
  is already covered by A-008's frozen suite, so all of this runs headless.
- Property (deterministic): AC-2 swept over every correct/wrong/timeout prefix up to a documented
  finite bound derived from the shortest guided victory, asserting `playerHull > 0` and
  `phase !== 'defeat'` after every transition. Separately drive enough correct volleys to require
  `victory`. A hand-picked case would miss the pattern that sinks them.
- Visual (design-fidelity, NOT frozen): the coaching affordance's appearance against the named
  board at 375pt and at 360×640 / 390×844 / 430×932, screenshot evidence attached.
  **Posture-gated deferral** — no component-test harness exists for the app layer
  (`.tdd-swarm/posture.md`).
