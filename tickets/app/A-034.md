## Context

The live tray already honors each cannon's skill, timer, damage band, temperament, projectile, arc,
and spectacle. The user sees only two same-skill starters because acquisition is narrow, and no
explicit difficulty label explains the trade. Double Broadside's true two-volley action remains
engine ticket T-022 and must not be faked in presentation.

## Planning Decisions

- **[locked-decision]** Difficulty is derived from curriculum grade, fuse, damage band, and
  temperament; it never changes the authored question or invents an unrelated score.
- **[locked-decision]** Projectile/spectacle is presentation only and all catalog cannons must have
  a complete mapping.
- **[locked-decision]** D-6 is narrowed for demo-ready placement: K-1 keeps the two starters;
  grade 2-3 additionally receives the Six-Pounder; grade 4-5 additionally receives the
  Twelve-Pounder. Every other range cannon remains mastery-earned. This makes the starting tray
  age-appropriate without erasing the practice loop.
- **[locked-decision]** These are explicit older-band placement exceptions, not a rewrite of their
  catalog `range` unlock. Six-Pounder/Twelve-Pounder therefore have two intentional acquisition
  paths for their approved bands; A-032 audits that exact policy. OWNER-RULINGS records this
  owner-authorized D-6 supersession.
- **[deferred]** True Double-Shot is T-022 plus a later app adapter; this ticket labels it
  unavailable until that engine contract is integrated rather than claiming it works.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given any owned cannon, when shown in the tray or gun deck, then its math skill,
  child-readable difficulty, fuse, damage band, temperament, and special weapon identity are
  truthful and accessible.
- **AC-2**: Given every catalog cannon, when presentation coverage is audited, then each has a
  distinct complete projectile/arc record and no generic fallback.
- **AC-3**: Given two cannons with different skills or risk, when fired, then the selected cannon's
  own question, timer, recoil, projectile, and arc are used.
- **AC-4**: Given a cannon whose special mechanic is not implemented, when rendered, then it is not
  labeled enabled and no visual copy claims extra damage/volleys.
- **AC-5**: Given each grade band, when placement completes,
  then the equipped starting set contains playable curriculum at the approved starting level and
  never an above-band cannon.
- **AC-6**: Given the acquisition-policy audit and owner rulings, then the only direct
  non-starter placement grants are Six-Pounder for grades 2–3 and Twelve-Pounder for grades 4–5,
  and their original mastery paths remain functional.

## Test Plan

- Source guard: no presentation-only field enters engine damage.
- Native evidence: starter, standard, and spectacle weapon in tray and flight.
