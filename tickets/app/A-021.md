## Context

The rebuilt chart has now passed its narrow first-frame and route smoke in A-018. The release still
needs one uninterrupted native proof of the child-facing loop: onboarding, duel, timeout, victory,
reward persistence, chart return, and practice. This is a release-verification ticket over already
shipped behavior. Its Test Agent records simulator evidence and does not edit production or test
files. A failed check creates a narrow fix ticket with its own frozen reproducer before any
implementer is dispatched.

## Required Release Evidence

The one deterministic contract is frozen before native verification:

- **AC-1**: Given the four shipped emoji-exposure call sites identified by the native audit, when
  their rendered source strings are inspected, then the duel-HUD anchor (`U+2693`) and all three
  directional-triangle sites (`U+25B6`/`U+25C0`) carry `U+FE0E` text presentation so the native UI
  does not silently substitute platform-color emoji.

The remaining checks are device observations rather than numbered frozen-test criteria. The app
layer has no component/e2e harness, and the ticket authorizes no implementation.

- On an iOS simulator synced to the exact `app/shell` commit, a fresh install reaches the chart
  through grade picker, captain name, flag choice, and the guided-duel route.
- The duel-HUD anchor and all three shipped directional-triangle sites render as text rather than
  platform-color emoji, matching the exact four call sites fixed in `8ee28eb`.
- Fight opens a real duel, at least one answer resolves, and an unanswered fuse reaches the
  “Damp powder” timeout panel without changing either hull or the answered-question counters.
- The rival can be sunk, victory returns to chart, the earned coin total persists, and the chart
  fog/progression state remains coherent.
- Practice opens the gunnery range and can return to chart.
- After force-closing and relaunching the app, the captain resumes on the expected route with their
  persisted name, flag, coins, and progress.
- Timestamped screenshots, runtime notes, exact commit, simulator model/OS, and any console/runtime
  warnings are stored under `.tdd-swarm/release-evidence/A-021/`.

## Test Plan

- Static regression (deterministic, frozen): AC-1 scans the exact app-layer anchor and directional
  triangle call sites and proves every sequence includes `U+FE0E`.
- Native E2E (release evidence): one uninterrupted simulator run through every observation above.
- State audit (release evidence): record counters/hulls around timeout and coins/progress before and
  after relaunch.
- Runtime audit (release evidence): record red-screen, worklet, and Metro errors or confirm none.
- Independent review (process): a reviewer checks the evidence against every required observation.
