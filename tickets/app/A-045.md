## Why

A-013 replaced the duel's composed ships with pre-rendered Kenney hull PNGs. The result no longer
looked like the game: the player's wood-and-parchment sloop with its red-striped sails and the
rival's dark purple hull both became grey-brown raster boats, and the layered sail/wake/luff motion
went with them.

Two independent reasons it was wrong, either one sufficient:

1. **The boards compose their ships.** Neither artifact contains a side-view ship raster. The duel
   prototype's own footnote reads: _"Ships are grey-box stand-ins; cannonball, blast and fire are
   the real Kenney CC0 sprites."_ The composition **is** the design, not a placeholder for it.
2. **The seventeen files A-013 added appear in neither artifact.** Verified by hashing every PNG in
   both artifact bundles against every PNG in `assets/sprites/`: seven hulls, six flags, two crew
   and two dinghies matched nothing.

The owner had already cut A-013 from scope; it shipped anyway.

## Owner ruling — D-10, 2026-07-29

> "we no longer want to use any of the kenny cc0 or any other of the items, we only want exactly
> what comes from the claude design artifacts"

**Sources of truth, and the only ones:**

- Duel prototype — `https://claude.ai/code/artifact/541ddd21-4ffd-425c-be57-8b6da70de66a`
- Interface design boards — `https://claude.ai/code/artifact/fbcdb054-e466-4f05-8175-4307a1ba7581`

**Where code, tickets or asset packs conflict with a board, the board wins.**

### Interpretation, flagged for the owner

"No Kenney" and "exactly what the artifacts contain" are in tension, because the artifacts
themselves embed nine Kenney rasters and label them as such. Resolved by **provenance, not
pedigree**: a raster ships if and only if it is byte-identical to an image embedded in one of the
two artifacts. That rule deletes every file the owner objected to and keeps the app matching the
boards. A literal zero-raster reading would require composing the explosions and cannonball too,
which would make the app diverge _from_ the prototype — the opposite of the ask. **Say so if the
literal reading was intended.**

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1 — Provenance.** Every `.png` under `assets/sprites/` is byte-identical to an image embedded
  in one of the two artifacts. Nine files qualify; the seventeen A-013 added are deleted from disk
  and from the manifest. No `hullSpriteForKind` or `flagSpriteForId` survives.
- **AC-2 — Composition.** `Ship.tsx` builds hull, masts, yard, sails, deck rail and posts, stern
  castle, bowsprit, pennant and gunports from the board's own coordinates. The only raster it may
  reference is `sprite.fire`, which is what the board itself draws on a burning hull.
- **AC-3 — Sail correction (board 7a).** Topsail and mainsail carry broad vertical stripes
  (`#D93A2E` on `#FFF6E4`); the jib stays plain "so the silhouette does not turn into noise at
  26px". Enemies carry no stripe.
- **AC-4 — A-031 preserved.** Crossbones, skull, shark fin, ghost opacity/glow and the kraken form
  still render, as overlays on the composed ship rather than hull swaps.
- **AC-5 — Pennant.** The onboarding flag becomes the pennant **colour** on a shaped flag, never a
  raster id. `pennantFlagId` is gone.
- **AC-6 — Motion.** Bob, wake pulse and staggered sail luff survive at the board's timings; the
  SeaStage shot/impact/splash sequence is untouched.
- **AC-7 — Rival identity (board 7b).** The pirate sloop is the board's purple sloop, not the brown
  ship A-031 invented. Every enemy is a re-tint of one shell — the board's own words: _"everything
  else is a re-tint of this."_
- **AC-8 — Docs.** `design/boards/README.md`, `design/DESIGN-BRIEF.md`, `assets/README.md`,
  `assets/source/README.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 and `PLAN.md` no longer point agents at the asset
  packs.

## Planning decisions

- **The sea chart keeps `ship-01.png`.** It is byte-identical to the image the corrections board's
  Sea chart screen authors as `<img … width:42px>`, and it is a _top-down_ map boat — the duel's
  side-view composition would be wrong there. Removing it would diverge from the board. This
  contradicts the literal instruction "remove ship rasters from the runtime ship path" and is called
  out here rather than done silently.
- **The hull polygon is `0,0 100,0 90,100 9,100`,** not the `88,100 10,100` named in the brief. The
  brief quoted the pre-A-013 code; the board says 90/9. Boards win.
- **One geometry, two palettes.** The board authors the rival at its own 126×106 box with two
  gunports and no rail posts or bowsprit. `Ship.tsx` renders the player's geometry scaled to 126pt
  and carries rival identity in palette plus the tattered sail and jagged pennant outlines. A
  deliberate ~95%-of-the-read trade against the deadline, recorded in
  `design/fixtures/ship-prototype.json`.
- **`assets/source/` (26 MB) is left on disk,** marked decommissioned in its README. Deleting
  downloads is the owner's call; nothing imports it and the AC-1 test prevents it leaking back in.
