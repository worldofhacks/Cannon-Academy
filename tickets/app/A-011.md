## Context

By the Grandline a captain owns up to ten cannons and the duel tray holds three. Nothing decides
which three. T-030 is the engine rule; this is the screen that drives it.

It already bites at MVP scale. Under owner ruling D-6, `resolvePlacement('k_1')` now returns **two**
starters — but `app/duel.tsx` still builds its tray from `resolvePlacement` directly rather than from
the captain's `equippedCannons`, so nothing a player chooses can reach the duel. Board 4d is the
designed answer.

**The one-line tray fix has already landed** and is not part of this ticket's remaining work:
`app/duel.tsx` now builds its tray from `captain.equippedCannons` rather than from
`resolvePlacement('k_1')`, so the hardcoded band is gone and a chosen loadout can reach the duel.
What is left here is the screen that lets a player choose it.

**Unblocked 2026-07-29.** Engine **T-035** shipped `TRAY_CAPACITY` in `src/engine/tuning.ts`.
App/shell has merged `swarm/engine-core` through that tip. Implement against the frozen suite in
`.worktrees/wt-A-011`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1**: Given more owned cannons than slots, when the deck opens, then every owned cannon is
  shown and exactly the equipped ones are marked.
- **AC-2**: Given three equipped cannons, when a fourth is equipped, then the player must displace
  one — the deck never silently drops a choice.
- **AC-3**: Given an equipped set, when it is committed, then it persists and the duel tray shows
  exactly it, in catalog order.
- **AC-4**: Given an attempt to equip zero cannons, when committed, then it is refused.
- **AC-5**: Given a newly unlocked cannon, when the deck opens, then it is marked as new until seen.
